PawAlert privacy statement

Version 2.0 · Last updated: 28 October 2026

This translation is provided for convenience. If there is any difference, the Dutch version prevails.

1. Who is responsible?

Bart Willems (PawAlert) is responsible for processing your personal data in PawAlert.
Contact for privacy questions: Pawalert@outlook.com

2. What data do we process?

Account details (name, email address, account type; for professional accounts also the name of the practice or organisation, municipality, phone number and company number, if provided); details about your pets (name, type, breed, sex, colour, date of birth, description) and photos; missing-report and sighting data (for a missing report possibly a phone number, if you enter it yourself), with municipality and, if you give consent, GPS location; your choice to receive notifications and the notifications we send you in the app.

3. Logging in with Google or Facebook

If you choose to log in with Google or Facebook, we receive your name and email address from that service. We do not receive your password or other data from that service. Google's and Facebook's own privacy terms also apply when you use them.

4. Why do we process this data?

To manage accounts, show missing and found pets, link sightings to reports, make contact possible, and prevent abuse or technical problems. The legal basis is performing the service you use, your consent (for example for location) and our legitimate interest in keeping the service safe.

5. What is public?

What you place in a public missing report or sighting (pet's name, photo, description, municipality, place where the pet was seen and a sighting on the map) may be visible to other users. On the map and in the public list, the location of a missing report is rounded (about 500 m), so your home address is not visible exactly. What you write yourself in text fields is shown as you entered it. Your email address and login details are not shown publicly. Do not put unnecessary personal data in free text fields.

If you do not use GPS for a missing report or sighting, we look up the centre of the municipality you entered via OpenStreetMap and keep that as the place for the map. This way visitors see dots on the map without your exact address being known. A dot for a missing pet shows the photo, name, type and the last known place that the owner entered themselves.

5b. Notifications about possible matches

As the owner of a missing pet you can give consent for notifications in the app. If an animal that may look like yours is seen later (same animal type, nearby, after your report), we add a notification to your account with the details of that sighting. We compare type, place and time. PawAlert never decides whether it is your pet: you judge that yourself. Without your consent we do not send notifications. You can withdraw your consent at any time in the My notifications screen.

5c. Comparing photos

Anyone who sees an animal can have their photo compared with the photos of public missing reports. That comparison happens in your own browser, on your own device. Your photo is not sent to us or to any other party for this. For the comparison, an image model is downloaded from an external server (jsDelivr and Google). The photos of the missing pets are already public. The result is only a suggestion: nobody is linked automatically, the person who saw the animal decides.

5d. Private chat

Anyone who reports a sighting can tick that the owner of the missing pet may try to make contact. Only the owner can then start a private chat. In that chat you do not see each other's name, email address or phone number, only the role (owner or observer). The messages are not public: only the two participants can read them. What you write in a message is up to you; so do not share an address or phone number unless you trust the other person. We keep the messages for 12 months at most, or until the missing report or your account is deleted.

5e. Phone number with a missing report (optional)

With a missing report you can voluntarily enter a phone number, so that people who have seen your pet can reach you quickly. That number does not appear in the public list, on the map or on the poster. It is only visible to logged-in PawAlert users, after they click to show it. The legal basis is your consent; you can delete the number at any time via Edit missing report. Anyone who gets to see the number can also use it outside PawAlert. So only enter a number on which you are happy to be called. If the missing report is deleted or marked as found, the number is no longer visible to other users. The number is kept together with the missing report as described under point 7.

6. Who processes data for us?

Supabase (authentication, database and storage of photos), GitHub Pages (hosting of the website), OpenStreetMap (map display and looking up your municipality via your location, if you choose that) and, if you choose that, Google or Facebook (logging in).

7. How long do we keep data?

We keep your account data as long as your account exists. If you delete your account or ask for deletion, we erase your data within 30 days. An ongoing missing report is kept until you delete it or mark it as found. Closed reports and sightings are kept for 12 months at most, after which they are erased or anonymised. Notifications in the app are kept until you delete them or the related missing report is erased.

8. Your rights

You can ask for access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability or object to processing, and you can withdraw your consent. To do so, send an email to Pawalert@outlook.com. You can also lodge a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority (dataprotectionauthority.be).

9. Having your data deleted

Do you want your account and all linked data (pets, photos, notifications, sightings) to be deleted? Email Pawalert@outlook.com with the email address of your account. This also applies if you logged in via Google or Facebook.

10. Cookies and advertising

PawAlert only uses what is needed to keep you logged in. Non-essential cookies, analytics or personalised advertising are only activated after separate consent.
